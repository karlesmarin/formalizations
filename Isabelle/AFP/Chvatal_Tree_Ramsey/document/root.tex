\title{Chv\'atal's Theorem on the Ramsey Number of a Triangle versus a Tree}
\author{Carles Mar\'in}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We formalize Chv\'atal's theorem~\cite{Chvatal1977}: for every tree $T$ with $n$ vertices, the graph Ramsey number $R(K_3,T)$ equals $2n-1$. The upper bound is proved by induction on $n$, removing a leaf; the lower bound is the complete bipartite graph with parts of sizes $n-1$ and $n-1$. As a corollary, every connected graph with $n$ vertices and at most $n-1$ edges, being a tree, has the same Ramsey number. The statements follow those of Erd\H{o}s problem 1182 in the formal-conjectures repository.
\end{abstract}
