\title{Two Theorems of Erd\H{o}s, Rubin and Taylor on Choosability}
\author{Carles Mar\'in}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We formalize two results of Erd\H{o}s, Rubin and Taylor~\cite{ERT1980} on list colourings. First, the least number of vertices of a bipartite graph that is not $2$-choosable is $6$: $K_{3,3}$ is not $2$-choosable, and every bipartite graph with at most five vertices is. Second, the complete multipartite graph $K_{2,\dots,2}$ with $k$ parts of size two is $k$-choosable, by induction on $k$ and Hall's theorem from the entry on the marriage theorem. The statements follow Erd\H{o}s problem 629 and the Erd\H{o}s--Rubin--Taylor variant of Ohba's conjecture in the formal-conjectures repository.
\end{abstract}
