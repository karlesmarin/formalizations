\title{The Core of Dunn and Pretty's Triangle-Halving Deltoid}
\author{Carles Mar\'in}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Dunn and Pretty~\cite{DunnPretty1972} showed that the lines halving the area of a triangle envelope a
deltoid bounded by three hyperbola arcs, of area $(\frac34\ln 2-\frac12)$ times that of the triangle. In the reference
triangle we formalize the core: a line through the two legs at a vertex halves the area iff it is tangent to the
hyperbola $xy=1/8$; the cusps; the integrals of the three curved segments ($\frac{3}{32}-\frac{\ln 2}{8}$ each); and
the final value. The affine reduction and the identification of the deltoid region with the cusp triangle minus the
three segments are explained but not formalized.
\end{abstract}
